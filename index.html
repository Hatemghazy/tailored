Know your size in every brand before you order.

tAIlored measures your feet with your phone camera and a bank card, then tells you the size each brand actually makes for you.

Try the interactive prototype Join the waitlist

This is a concept prototype. The scan is simulated, so nothing uses your camera and no real orders are placed.

A 43 in one brand can be a 44 in another.

Every brand builds its shoes on its own mould, so the number on the box means something different each time. Online you can't try before you buy, so you guess, order two sizes, or send them back.

One foot, 263 mm Brand A EU 42.5 Brand B EU 43⅓ Brand C EU 44

Size charts disagree

Each brand converts foot length to a size differently. The gap is often a whole size.

Width is ignored

Most charts only look at length, so wide and narrow feet get the same answer.

Returns cost days

Another courier visit, another wait, and the pair you wanted may be gone.

Thirty seconds, three steps.

No tape measure and no extra hardware. Just your phone and a card from your wallet.

  1. 1

    Put a bank card beside your foot

    A card's size is fixed, so it turns pixels into millimetres. An A4 sheet or a 1 EGP coin also works.

  2. 2

    Scan each foot

    Hold the phone over one foot, then the other. You get length, width and arch height.

  3. 3

    See your size in every brand

    Each size comes with a confidence score, and the shop shows only styles in stock in your size.

Built to say when it isn't sure.

A wrong size is worse than no size. So tAIlored shows how confident it is, and asks you to rescan instead of guessing.

A confidence score on every scan

Below 80%, the app tells you why (low light, a shaky hand, carpet) and asks you to scan again.

80%

It learns from how they fit

Say a pair felt snug, and every future size in that brand adjusts for you.

Width counts

A wide foot gets room from a longer size, the way a good shop fitter would do it.

Shop only what fits

Filter every brand to styles that are in stock in your size, and pay cash on delivery, by card or with InstaPay.

Sign in with your phone number

A 6-digit code instead of a password. Or skip it and browse as a guest.

Your feet are personal. The app is designed around that.

Foot images are biometric data, and many people are uneasy photographing their feet. These are product requirements, not afterthoughts.

Processed on your phone

Camera frames are deleted as soon as your measurement is saved. Only the numbers are kept.

Yours to export or delete

Download everything as a file, or delete your measurements or your whole account in one step.

Built for Egypt's PDPL and GDPR

Sharing anonymised size data with brands is off by default, and only you can turn it on.

How this was built

tAIlored is a product concept by Hatem Ghazy, a Senior Product Owner based in Cairo. It's a working case study in taking an idea from requirements to something people can hold in their hand.

The AI measurement model isn't built yet. Everything around it is designed and working: the flows, the edge cases, the sizing logic and the privacy controls.

LinkedIn Behance GitHub
  1. Business requirementsMarket, segments and functional requirements, including the 80% confidence rule.
  2. User research in FigJamPersonas, empathy maps, a customer journey map and the full sign-in flowchart.
  3. Design system in Figma28 components with 133 variants, Arabic-ready type and dark mode.
  4. Interactive prototype32 screens, a live sizing engine and every edge case clickable.

tAIlored is a concept product. Brand names in the prototype belong to their owners and are used only to demonstrate sizing; there is no affiliation. No real orders, payments or measurements are processed.
